Why missed LSA calls cost more than one job

Every Google Local Services Ads call is a lead you paid for. When nobody picks up, you lose that customer, and you also hurt the responsiveness signal Google uses to rank LSA advertisers. Google says plainly that missed calls can hurt responsiveness.

Contractors miss calls for normal reasons: the crew is on a roof, the office is on another line, or it’s 9 PM on a Sunday. A generic answering service or voicemail doesn’t fix the ranking problem. It also doesn’t give you what you need to call the customer back quickly.

The stakes are going up. As LSA moves into Google Ads, industry reports say a missed call during your business hours can now be charged as a lead if the caller stays on the line long enough. Read our guide to LSA’s move into Performance Max for details.

How LSA Command answers every call: humans first, AI always

  1. 0–15 seconds: your team gets first shot. Every LSA call rings your phones first. If someone answers, it’s a normal call and the AI stays out of the way.
  2. At 15 seconds: AI picks up. If nobody answers (busy, on a job, after hours), the LSA Command AI answers on the spot. It qualifies the caller, captures the job details, and books the job or warm-transfers a hot lead to your phone while the caller is still on the line.
  3. Inside 30 seconds: Google sees the call answered. Every call is answered inside Google’s 30-second window, so your response rate stays at 100%.
  4. Instantly: you get the full picture. A text and an email hit your phone with the caller’s number, the call recording, a five-second AI summary, and the full transcript. Google’s own alert for a phone lead is just an email with no recording or transcript.

Robocalls never reach your phone

Paid LSA numbers attract spam. LSA Command runs a three-layer defense that stops robocalls before your phone rings and counts every block in your dashboard. Your team spends its time on real customers.

Open 24 hours, without a night shift

Because every call and message is answered, you can list your business as Open 24 Hours in LSA. Being “open now” when competitors are closed earns you placement at nights, on weekends and on holidays. Those are often the searches where homeowners have an urgent problem and will book whoever answers.

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI answer calls my team could have taken?

No. Every call rings your team first for 15 seconds. The AI only answers when no one picks up.

Does answering every call improve my Google LSA ranking?

Responsiveness is one of the factors Google uses to rank Local Services Ads, and Google says missed calls can hurt it. Answering every call within 30 seconds protects that signal. Ranking also depends on proximity, reviews, and business hours, so no tool can promise a specific position.

What do I receive after the AI handles a call?

A text and an email with the caller’s number, the call recording, an AI summary, and the full transcript, so you can call back already knowing what the customer needs.

Can the AI transfer a hot lead to me?

Yes. Qualified callers can be warm-transferred straight to your phone while they’re still on the line.
