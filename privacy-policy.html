Privacy Policy

How LSA Command protects your information

LSA Command is committed to protecting the privacy of all visitors, customers, and partners worldwide. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have about your data.

Scope

This policy applies to all individuals who access our website or contact us through the site, regardless of location. It covers information collected directly, automatically, and from third-party services.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: phone numbers, email addresses, and business information provided when you inquire or request support.
  • Usage data: pages visited, links clicked, referrer URL, and other website interactions.
  • Technical data: browser type, device model, operating system, IP address, and site performance metrics.
  • Communications: messages you send us via phone, SMS, or forms.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries, provide information, and support customer requests.
  • Personalize and improve our website, content, and offers.
  • Analyze traffic, detect issues, and enhance site security.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, and analyze usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Some cookies are essential for the site to work properly.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers who assist with hosting, analytics, communications, or legal compliance. These providers are required to protect your information and may only use it for the purposes we specify.

Data retention and transfers

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Information may be stored and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

Your rights and choices

  • Request access to the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your information where permitted by law.
  • Opt out of marketing communications where applicable.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

Children

Our website is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
