01

WHITE-LABEL AGENCY ACCOUNT

Make the platform your own.

Create your agency account, add your brand, connect your domain, and get your email ready.

VIDEO WALKTHROUGH · 8:16Watch on YouTube ↗
01Create your agency account+

Open the agency signup page and enter your first and last name, agency name, phone number, email, and password. Choose Create Account to start onboarding, then confirm your agency name and select Save and continue.

Create your agency account ↗
02Add your agency branding+

Upload your desktop logo, mobile logo, and browser favicon. The favicon is the small icon shown in the browser tab. Preview your images and continue. You can update your branding later in settings.

03Connect your white-label domain+

Choose an address such as app.youragency.com. Use a domain you already own, or register one with a provider such as GoDaddy or Namecheap first. The examples here are placeholders—use your own domain.

In the DNS provider that manages your domain’s nameservers, add this record for your chosen subdomain:

TypeA
Name / Hostapp (for app.youragency.com)
Value / Points to34.200.70.126
TTLAutomatic or provider default

Use the IP shown in your onboarding screen if it differs. Add a subdomain record; keep the records serving your existing website and email intact.

GoDaddy

Domain Portfolio → your domain → DNS → Add New Record. Select A, enter the values above, and save.

GoDaddy instructions ↗

Namecheap

Domain List → Manage → Advanced DNS → Host Records → Add New Record. Select A Record, enter the values, and save.

Namecheap instructions ↗

Back in LSA Command, enter the full address, such as app.youragency.com, and select Save web address. DNS and SSL setup can take time. You can continue onboarding while it connects, then return to check the status and open your branded login. Contact support if it is still not ready after 24 hours.

04Set up outgoing email (SMTP)+

Use a real mailbox on your agency’s domain, such as notifications@youragency.com, to send branded emails. Enter your agency name as the sender name and your mailbox as the sender email.

Sending with Google Workspace

SMTP hostsmtp.gmail.com
Port587
UsernameYour full mailbox email address
PasswordA Google app password
EncryptionSelect SSL / TLS in LSA Command (port 587 uses STARTTLS)
  1. Sign in to the Google account for the sending mailbox.
  2. Enable 2-Step Verification in the account’s Security settings.
  3. Open Google App passwords ↗, name the app “LSA Command,” and generate a password.
  4. Paste the generated password into your SMTP password field. Keep it private.
  5. Select Verify and save email settings, then send a test email and check that it arrives.

App passwords may be unavailable under your Workspace administrator’s security policy. Ask your administrator or use another supported SMTP provider. See Google’s app password help and SMTP settings.

Using another email provider?

Enter that provider’s SMTP host, port, username, password, and encryption settings instead. Save and send a test email before continuing.

05Choose how client access requests are branded+

For Google LSA access requests to come from your agency’s identity, create a dedicated Google Workspace user, such as connect@youragency.com, and coordinate access with the LSA Command team through support.

In Google Admin, go to Directory → Users → Add new user and set up the dedicated account with the appropriate Workspace license. Contact support to arrange the connection; do not send passwords through this guide.

This account is separate from your outgoing SMTP configuration. If you do not provide a dedicated Workspace account, client CID access requests will come from LSA Command.

Your agency foundation is ready.

Choose Add your first business in onboarding to begin the next walkthrough.

02

ADDING A NEW BUSINESS

Meet your first client workspace.

Add a client, teach the AI about their business, and decide how calls, messages, and notifications should work.

VIDEO WALKTHROUGH · 11:55Watch on YouTube ↗
All nine steps are available. Work through each one, or choose Skip for now for something you are not ready to configure. Return to skipped settings later; skipping does not enable a feature.
01Confirm the business details+

Enter your client’s contact name and email, then search for their business to populate its name, main keyword, and address. If it is not found, enter the information manually. You can optionally create a client login. Save, then review the business details in the onboarding journey.

02Define the right leads+

Enter the client’s website and describe their services, service area, and exclusions. Select Write the rules for me, then review and edit the suggested rules. Check both high-value lead criteria and Google survey criteria—they serve different purposes. For example, a roofer may want repair inquiries but not new roof installations. Make sure the rules reflect the actual business; a refund is not guaranteed.

03Set up the AI phone number+

Open the phone-number step, search for an available number, and review the purchase details before purchasing. Confirm the selected number is attached to the business. You will use it for the phone and messaging setup that follows.

04Create the AI agent+

Choose the agent’s goal, such as gathering information and transferring qualified calls. Explain when a human should take over and what information the agent should collect. Add the client’s website, select Write my AI instructions, and review the opening message and generated prompt. Adjust the instructions so they accurately represent the business.

05Submit the client’s LSA CID+

Enter the client’s Google Local Services Ads customer ID (CID) and select Submit and continue. The client must accept the resulting access request before LSA Command can access their leads and recordings and submit surveys and notes. Requests come from your agency’s connected identity or from LSA Command, depending on your Workspace setup.

06Configure call handling+

Choose whether calls go to AI first or ring a person first. Set the destination numbers, ring duration, simultaneous ringing if needed, and the fallback when nobody answers. Configure business hours and check the after-hours and weekend behavior. Test the route with a real call after saving.

07Install website chat+

Open the website chat step, copy the installation code, and add it to the client’s website. Use the widget settings to match the client’s branding and choose the appropriate display style. Visit the published website and send a test message to confirm it appears and responds as expected.

08Configure message replies+

After setting up the phone number, review the messaging agent’s instructions and business knowledge. Set how it should qualify new inquiries, answer questions, and hand conversations to a person or share a booking link. Review any follow-up behavior before enabling it, then test a conversation.

09Add lead notifications+

Enter the email addresses and phone numbers of the owner or staff who should receive new-lead notifications. Review the recipients, save your settings, and check delivery. You can revisit the business profile, call flow, AI agent, and notifications from the dashboard at any time.

Want to fine-tune your AI?

From the business dashboard, open the AI agent to review voice options, knowledge sources, transfer rules, follow-ups, booking links, and chat widget styling. The end of the second video walks through these settings.
