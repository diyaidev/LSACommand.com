The hidden problem with LSA message leads

When a homeowner messages you through Google Local Services Ads, your reply goes back to Google over SMS. Your phone, or any off-the-shelf AI agent, shows the reply as “delivered.”

But in the accounts we audit, 10–15% of those replies never land in the LSA lead thread and never reach the customer. This happens to replies from people and from generic AI bots alike. The only way to see it is to log into LSA and open each lead, so most businesses never find out.

Every dropped reply is a paid lead that went cold. It also counts against your responsiveness, which is part of how LSA ranking works.

How the LSA API fallback works

  1. Reply fast. LSA Command’s AI answers new message leads in under 30 seconds, day or night. You can also reply yourself; you’re backed up either way.
  2. Verify the thread. Because LSA Command is built on the LSA API, it checks the actual lead thread after every reply, not just the SMS delivery receipt.
  3. Force it through. If a reply hasn’t landed within minutes, LSA Command pushes it into the thread through the API.
  4. Count it. Every recovered message appears in your dashboard as an exact count, not an estimate.

Protect the “replies in minutes” badge

Fast, consistent replies help earn Google’s “typically replies in minutes” label on your listing. Slow ones can show customers that you reply in hours instead. A 90% response rate and a 100% response rate look similar on paper, but the gap is real paid leads that never got a reply.

Frequently asked questions

Why would a reply to an LSA message lead not be delivered?

Replies travel back to Google over an SMS bridge. A share of them never reach the LSA thread even though your phone shows them as delivered. Without checking the thread through the LSA API, there’s no way to know.

Do I have to let the AI answer my messages?

No. You can answer messages yourself and LSA Command will still verify that every reply reached the customer, re-sending any that drop.

How fast does LSA Command reply to a new message lead?

The AI replies in under 30 seconds, 24 hours a day.
