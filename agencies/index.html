What breaks when you run LSA for a book of clients

Missed calls you never see

Your client's crew is on a job, the call goes to voicemail, and their responsiveness signal drops. You find out when the leads slow down.

Message replies that vanish

Replies to LSA message leads travel over SMS, and some never land in the LSA thread. Nobody knows unless they open every lead.

Credits nobody tracks

Spam and invalid leads can be credited, but only if someone rates them and checks what Google actually credited.

Reports clients don't trust

"How many leads did we get, and what happened to them?" is hard to answer from the LSA dashboard alone.

What LSA Command runs for each client

Humans first, AI always

Calls ring the client's team first. At 15 seconds AI answers, qualifies, books or warm-transfers, and texts the recording, summary and transcript.

100% of message leads answered

An API watchdog checks the actual LSA thread after every reply and pushes it through if it didn't land.

Every lead rated to Google

Each lead gets a 1–5 quality rating back to Google, spam and invalid leads are flagged for credit, and credits are tracked.

Service area and reviews

Profitable cities and zips pushed to LSA, AI review follow-up after every job, and a rank tracker pinned on every city served.

White-label ready

Counted, not estimated

Robocalls blocked, messages recovered and lead quality, as exact numbers in the dashboard you show your client.
