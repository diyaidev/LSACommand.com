GDPR

Global data protection and privacy rights

This page explains how LSA Command handles personal data in a privacy-conscious way and how the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) may apply to individuals in the European Economic Area.

Our commitment

We are committed to protecting personal information regardless of where you live. For visitors in the EEA, we also comply with GDPR requirements when processing personal data.

What data we process

We may process contact details, inquiry messages, analytics data, and other information needed to respond to requests and improve our services.

Legal basis

Where GDPR applies, we process personal data only when we have a lawful basis, such as consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, or legal obligations.

Your rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Ask for deletion of personal data where permitted.
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Request restriction of processing or data portability where available.

International transfers

We may transfer data to countries outside the EEA. When we do, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or rely on applicable legal mechanisms to protect your information.

Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in our Privacy Policy, and as required by law.

How to exercise your rights

To exercise your rights or ask questions about this page, contact us at 989-712-4370. We will respond to inquiries as required by applicable law.

Additional privacy protections

Even if GDPR does not apply in your jurisdiction, we still aim to handle personal data responsibly and with respect for your privacy.
