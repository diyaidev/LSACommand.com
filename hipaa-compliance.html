Privacy-minded handling of sensitive information

This page describes our approach to protecting sensitive information, including how we handle personal and health-related data responsibly.

Our approach to security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

Sensitive information

While LSA Command is not a healthcare provider, we take extra care with any sensitive information that is shared with us. Access is limited to authorized personnel only.

Data minimization and retention

We collect only the information necessary to respond to your request or provide services. We retain data only as long as required for the purpose and in accordance with our privacy practices.

Third-party partners

We may work with trusted service providers for hosting, analytics, and communications. These partners are expected to maintain privacy and security measures consistent with applicable standards.

Not legal or compliance advice

This page is informational only and does not constitute legal advice or guarantee compliance with HIPAA or any other law. HIPAA obligations depend on the specific business relationship and the nature of the information processed.

Global applicability

Although HIPAA is a U.S. law, our principles of data protection apply to all visitors. We strive to handle sensitive information with care regardless of your location.
