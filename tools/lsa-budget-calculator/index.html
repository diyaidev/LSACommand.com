How to read this

  • The estimate is only as good as the cost per lead. Google doesn’t publish lead prices, and they vary by trade, market, job type and competition. Run it on the account’s own average, and rerun it after a few weeks of real data.
  • Booking rate moves the budget more than anything else. The “10 points lower” line shows how much. Fixing answering usually beats raising the budget.
  • Cost per booked job is the number to show the client, not cost per lead. It’s what one job costs to win.
  • The weekly budget is an average, not a cap. A busy week can spend more. The monthly max is the real ceiling.

Google’s budget math

  • Monthly max = average weekly budget × 30.4 ÷ 7. Google says spend never goes over it, and the ads stop for the rest of the month once it’s reached. Source: Google, how to edit your budget.
  • After the move into Google Ads, the weekly budget is divided by 7 to set a daily average budget, and monthly spend is capped at the daily average × 30.4. Manual max-per-lead bidding isn’t supported there. Source: Google Ads Help, LSA transition.
  • Maximize leads is Google’s recommended bid mode, with a budget for at least 10 leads a week. Source: Google, how bidding works.
  • Target CPA in Google Ads is recommended only for campaigns with more than 15 leads a month whose cost per lead is too high; Maximize conversions is the default. Source: Google Ads Help, bidding for local services.

Assumptions

  • Leads cost the average you enter. Real weeks swing around it.
  • Booking rate is the same at a higher budget. In practice, a client near capacity books a smaller share of extra leads.
  • Nothing you type leaves your browser. “Copy link” puts the numbers in the part of the link after the #, which isn’t sent to any server.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Google Local Services Ads budget calculator?

Google doesn’t publish lead prices, so it has no official calculator. A practical estimate is leads needed per week (jobs wanted ÷ booking rate) × expected cost per lead, using the account’s own numbers.

How is the LSA monthly max calculated?

Average weekly budget × 30.4 ÷ 7. Google says spend never goes over it. A $700 weekly budget gives a $3,040 monthly max.

What happens to the budget when LSA moves into Google Ads?

Google divides the historical weekly budget by 7 to set a daily average budget and caps monthly spend at the daily average × 30.4. The monthly ceiling works out the same; the number the client sees changes.
