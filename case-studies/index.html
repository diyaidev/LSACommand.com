What LSA Command changed in these accounts

Between December and January, under-30-second response in these accounts jumped from 18%–40% to 68%–93%. LSA Command does four things on every account, and none of them depend on the client's team changing how it works.

  1. 01

    Every call answered inside 30 seconds

    The client's team rings first. If nobody picks up, AI answers at 15 seconds, so Google never logs a missed call against the ranking.

  2. 02

    Every message lead answered, and verified

    Replies go out in seconds and are confirmed through the LSA API, so none of them silently fail to deliver.

  3. 03

    Every lead rated back to Google

    Each lead is scored and rated, which teaches Google what a good lead looks like for this business. Spam and invalid leads get flagged for credit.

  4. 04

    Every result counted

    The account's response rate, lead quality, and lead volume are tracked monthly. Those are the same three numbers in these case studies, ready to send to the client.

How agencies run this across a client roster

The results above came from one system applied the same way to each account. Here is how an agency runs it for every LSA client it manages.

  1. Baseline each client on three numbers

    Pull under-30-second response rate, lead quality score, and monthly lead volume for the last three to six months. Most accounts have one number that is clearly broken, and it is usually response rate.

  2. Fix response first

    Turn on the call flow and message-lead replies. In these case studies, response rate was the first number to move and the one the client noticed fastest.

  3. Rate every lead

    Lead quality improves as Google learns from the ratings. It moves more slowly than response rate, so set that expectation with the client up front.

  4. Report monthly, under your brand

    Send each client the same three-number report every month. A dip like Champion Pools' February shows up while it is still a small fix, not a cancellation.

  5. Turn the results into your own case studies

    Once a client approves, the before-and-after becomes proof for your next pitch, in the format you're reading now.

Frequently asked questions

Are these real LSA Command clients?

Yes. Advantage Pools Bay Area, Champion Pools, and Edgewater Pool and Spa Services are pool contractors whose results are already published on our homepage. Each case study links to the original monthly report.

Isn't pool demand seasonal?

Yes, and we don't hide it. December is a slow month for pool companies, so part of the lead-volume jump into spring is seasonal. That is why each case study reports response rate and lead quality next to volume. Those two numbers don't rise just because it's spring, and they moved the most.

Why is April only a partial month?

The reports were pulled on April 23, 2026, so April covers roughly three weeks. We show March as the last full month and label April as month-to-date everywhere it appears.

Can my agency run LSA Command under its own brand?

Yes. LSA Command is white-label ready: you run the system for your clients under your brand, and lead alerts and reports go to you and your clients. Book an agency walkthrough to see the partner setup.

Will you publish more case studies?

Yes. We only publish a client's numbers after the client and their agency approve it. More agency-run accounts are in that approval queue now.

How these numbers are measured

Under-30s response rate
Share of the month's LSA leads answered within 30 seconds, the window Google uses for responsiveness.
Lead quality score
The account's average lead rating for the month on a five-point scale. Higher means more of the jobs the business actually wants.
Lead volume
LSA leads received in the month. April 2026 is month-to-date through April 23.
Source
Each case study links to the original monthly report. Combined figures are sums (volume) or simple averages across the three accounts (response rate and quality).
