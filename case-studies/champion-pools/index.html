What changed

The number that moved first was response speed. Under-30-second response went from 36% in November and 40% in December to 81% in January, then 98% in March and 100% in April. These are the pieces LSA Command runs on the account so that speed doesn't depend on someone grabbing the phone:

  • Call flow: the team rings first and AI answers at 15 seconds, so no call misses Google's 30-second window.
  • Message-lead replies: sent in seconds and verified through the LSA API.
  • Lead ratings: every lead scored and rated back to Google, with spam and invalid leads flagged for credit.

Champion Pools also ran Convertly for rapid lead engagement alongside LSA Command over this period, as its original report notes.

Lead quality followed more slowly, from 2.50 in December to 3.12 in March and 3.30 in April. That is the pattern to expect: responsiveness changes right away, and quality builds as Google learns from the ratings.

What agencies should take from it

Champion Pools shows that progress is not a straight line. Response rate dipped in February before reaching 97.6% in March and 100% in April. A monthly three-number report lets an agency catch that kind of dip early, while it is still easy to fix.

Frequently asked questions

What changed for Champion Pools?

Response speed. In December 2025, 40% of its LSA leads were answered in under 30 seconds. By April 2026 it was 100%, and lead volume and lead quality rose over the same months.

How much of the lead growth is seasonal?

Some of it. December is a slow month for pool companies. Response rate and lead quality don't rise just because spring arrives, which is why we report all three numbers together.
