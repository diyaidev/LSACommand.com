What changed

The number that moved first was response speed. Under-30-second response went from 20% in November and 27% in December to 68% in January, then 98% in March and 100% in April. These are the pieces LSA Command runs on the account so that speed doesn't depend on someone grabbing the phone:

  • Call flow: the team rings first and AI answers at 15 seconds, so no call misses Google's 30-second window.
  • Message-lead replies: sent in seconds and verified through the LSA API.
  • Lead ratings: every lead scored and rated back to Google, with spam and invalid leads flagged for credit.

Lead quality followed more slowly, from 2.45 in December to 3.69 in March and 4.19 in April. That is the pattern to expect: responsiveness changes right away, and quality builds as Google learns from the ratings.

What agencies should take from it

Edgewater is the quality story. Volume grew, but the bigger change is the kind of lead Google sent once every lead was answered fast and rated back. For an agency, quality is what keeps a client renewing after the volume novelty wears off.

Frequently asked questions

What changed for Edgewater Pools?

Response speed. In December 2025, 27% of its LSA leads were answered in under 30 seconds. By April 2026 it was 100%, and lead volume and lead quality rose over the same months.

How much of the lead growth is seasonal?

Some of it. December is a slow month for pool companies. Response rate and lead quality don't rise just because spring arrives, which is why we report all three numbers together.
