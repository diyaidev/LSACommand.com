How to Respond to Google LSA Message Leads (and Make Sure the Reply Arrives)

Message leads are a growing share of LSA volume, and they’re judged on speed. Here’s how to reply, and how to make sure your reply actually reaches the customer.

By LSA Command · May 28, 2026

On this page

  1. Reply in minutes, not hours
  2. What a good first reply includes
  3. The problem nobody sees: replies that never arrive
  4. Frequently asked questions

Reply in minutes, not hours

Homeowners message several businesses at once. The first useful reply usually wins. Fast replies also help earn Google’s “typically replies in minutes” label and protect your responsiveness score, which is part of how LSA ranks you.

What a good first reply includes

  1. Thank them and confirm the job (“Sounds like your AC is blowing warm air”).
  2. Ask the one or two questions you need to quote or schedule.
  3. Offer a specific next step: a call, a time slot, or a visit.
  4. Include a direct number to reach you.

The problem nobody sees: replies that never arrive

LSA message replies travel back to Google over SMS. Your phone shows them as delivered, but in the accounts we audit, 10–15% never land in the LSA thread. The customer never sees them and assumes you ignored them. The only way to catch this is to check each lead thread.

Frequently asked questions

How fast should I reply to an LSA message lead?

Within minutes. Homeowners message several providers at once, and responsiveness is an LSA ranking factor.

Why didn’t my customer get my LSA message reply?

Replies travel back to Google over SMS, and a share of them never reach the LSA thread even when your phone shows them as delivered. Check the thread in LSA, or use a tool that verifies delivery through the LSA API.
