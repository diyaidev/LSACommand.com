How to Rank Higher in Google Local Services Ads

Google ranks Local Services Ads on a handful of signals. Here’s what each one means, which ones you control, and how to move up.

What Google uses to rank LSA advertisers

Local Services Ads don’t use keywords or bids per click. Google decides which providers to show, and in what order, using a small set of signals. Its help center lists these as the main ranking factors:

  • Proximity to the person searching
  • Review score and number of reviews
  • Responsiveness to calls and messages
  • Business hours
  • Serious or repeated complaints about the business

Your budget and bid also determine how often you appear. Google’s own guide to improving LSA performance adds practical tips such as photos and selecting every job type you offer.

1. Responsiveness: the factor you control most

Responsiveness is the factor contractors can change fastest. Google says missed calls can hurt it, and slow message replies count too.

  • Answer calls inside 30 seconds, every time. LSA Command’s AI call answering rings your team first and picks up at 15 seconds if nobody answers.
  • Reply to message leads within minutes, and confirm the reply arrived. 10–15% of message replies can silently fail.
  • Watch your responsiveness score in the dashboard. It’s based on recent performance, so improvements show up quickly.

2. Reviews: rating and volume

Both your average rating and your review count matter, and homeowners compare them side by side. The fix is consistency: ask after every job, make posting easy, and reply to every review. See how automated review follow-up works.

3. Business hours

Being open when the customer searches matters. If you can genuinely answer around the clock, listing 24-hour availability lets you appear for night, weekend, and holiday searches when competitors are closed.

4. Proximity and your service area

You can’t move your shop, but you can choose where you show up. A focused service area keeps your budget on the cities and zip codes where you rank well and win profitable jobs. A rank tracker shows where you actually appear in each city.

5. Lead feedback and complaints

Rating every lead tells Google which leads you want. Fixing unhappy customers before they become complaints protects your standing. LSA Command rates every lead back to Google and routes unhappy customers to the owner.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most important Google LSA ranking factor?

Google doesn’t publish weights. Proximity, reviews, responsiveness, and business hours all matter. Responsiveness is usually the one a contractor can improve fastest.

Does answering calls faster improve LSA ranking?

Google lists responsiveness as a ranking factor and says missed calls can hurt it. Answering every call and message quickly protects that signal.

Can I pay to rank #1 in Local Services Ads?

Budget and bid affect how often you appear, but ranking also depends on proximity, reviews, responsiveness, and hours. No one can guarantee the top spot.
