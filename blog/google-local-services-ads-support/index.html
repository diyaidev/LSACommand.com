How to Contact Google Local Services Ads Support: The Agency Guide

Google runs several Local Services Ads support channels, and the right one depends on where the account is: still onboarding, live, or moving into Google Ads. Here’s which channel handles what, what to have ready, and how agencies keep support cases from stalling across a client book.

Nobody looks up the Local Services Ads (LSA) support number on a good day. You look it up because a client’s ads stopped, a verification is stuck, or a bill doesn’t make sense, and the client is asking you what’s going on.

The frustrating part is that LSA has more than one support channel, and calling the wrong one costs you a day. This guide lists every channel Google publishes, which problems each one handles, and how to prepare a case so it gets solved on the first contact. Phone numbers and hours below are from Google’s help pages as of November 1, 2026. Google changes these, so check the linked page before you dial.

What LSA support handles, and what it sends elsewhere

Google says the LSA support team “can only assist you with your Local Service Ads.” Two neighbouring problems go to different teams:

  • Google Ads questions (search campaigns, account-wide billing, Google Ads policy) go to the Google Ads Help Center and its contact options.
  • Business Profile problems (suspended profiles, name or address edits, verification of the listing itself) go to the Business Profile Help Center.

That split matters more after migration. Google says significant changes to a business name, storefront address or primary category trigger a verification review that typically takes 24 to 48 hours, and the campaign may pause while it runs. Source: Google Ads Help If a client’s ads paused right after someone edited their Business Profile, the fix may sit with the Business Profile team, not LSA.

What support can’t fix

Some of the most common client complaints aren’t support tickets.

Lead credits. Google now reassesses charged leads and issues credits automatically when it later judges a lead low quality, typically within 30 days. It no longer credits leads for “job type not serviced” or “geo not serviced,” and it asks you to rate each lead so it can send better-matched ones. Source: Google Local Services Help Calling support to argue a lead is rarely the fastest route. Rating every lead, and keeping job types and service areas tight so the wrong leads don’t arrive, is. Our bad leads guide covers prevention.

Missed-call charges. Google lists a missed business-hours call where the customer stays on the line more than 20 seconds as a chargeable lead. Source: Google Local Services Help That’s the rule working as written, not a billing error. See how to check a client’s bill for missed-call charges.

Ranking and lead volume. Support can confirm an account is active and eligible. It won’t tell you why a competitor ranks above your client. Ranking factors are covered in our guide to ranking higher.

Build the case file before you call

The fastest support calls are the ones where the agent never has to ask you to go find something. Have this ready:

  • The client’s customer ID and the email of the account administrator.
  • Account status: live, pre-badge, onboarding, migrated or migrating, and the migration date if you have the notice.
  • The exact problem, with dates: when it started, what changed just before, and what you’ve already checked.
  • Lead IDs and timestamps for any disputed or missing leads.
  • Screenshots of the status, the billing page or the error message.
  • Call recordings or message threads you want reviewed. After migration, Google stores call recordings and sensitive conversation history for up to 30 days, so download anything you might need for a case right away. Source: Google Ads Help
  • Any case number from earlier contact about the same issue.

How agencies should contact support for clients

A few habits keep support from turning into a black hole when you manage a dozen or more accounts.

Contact Google from a login that has access to the account. The contact form requires sign-in, and an agent can move faster when the person asking can see the account. Make sure your team has access through your manager account before you need it. [Ryan confirm: whether Google support has ever refused to discuss a client account with a manager-account user, and whether the account admin had to be on the call.]

One issue per case. A case that says “verification stuck, and also the bill looks wrong, and also leads dropped” gets routed to one team and the other two problems get lost. Open separate cases.

Log every case. Keep a simple shared sheet: client, date, channel, case number, problem, promised next step, follow-up date. With twenty clients, memory isn’t a system.

Know who receives Google’s emails. Google sends migration notices to the account administrator 14 days before migration, with a reminder 7 days later. Source: Google Ads Help After migration, lead email alerts go to all account users and direct managers by default, and at least one user has to stay opted in. If the administrator is the client’s personal inbox, the information support refers to may be sitting in an email you’ve never seen.

Use the Help Community for “is it just us?” When several clients show the same odd behavior at the same time, the Google Ads Help Community often shows whether it’s a wider issue before support confirms it.

Cut the number of support calls

Most LSA support calls trace back to a handful of preventable problems: a lead alert nobody saw, a message lead nobody answered, a Business Profile edit nobody logged, a verification email sitting unread. Build those checks into a weekly routine for every client and support becomes the exception.

Frequently asked questions

What is the phone number for Google Local Services Ads support?

For US and Canadian businesses that are new to LSA or onboarding, Google lists 1-877-824-3983, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern. Advertisers migrating to Performance Max have a dedicated line, +1-844-205-0011, with the same hours. Live accounts with the badge use the contact form.

Is Local Services Ads support the same as Google Ads support?

No. Google says the LSA support team can only help with Local Services Ads. Google Ads questions go to Google Ads support, and Business Profile problems go to the Business Profile Help Center.

Can an agency contact LSA support on a client’s behalf?

Agencies do it all the time. Use a Google login that has access to the client’s account, and have the customer ID and account details ready. Under Google’s third-party policy, the client can also get the customer ID from you and contact Google directly.

How do I dispute a bad Local Services Ads lead?

Rate the lead in the account. Google now issues credits automatically when it later judges a charged lead low quality, and it no longer credits leads for job types or areas the business doesn’t serve, so keep those settings accurate.

What hours is Local Services Ads support open?

Google lists Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern for US and Canada, and 9:00 to 18:00 UK time for its European phone lines.
