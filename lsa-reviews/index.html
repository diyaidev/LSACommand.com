Why reviews matter so much in LSA

Your review rating and review count are among the main things Google uses to rank Local Services Ads, alongside proximity, responsiveness, and business hours. They’re also what homeowners compare first when three providers show up side by side.

Most contractors know this and still don’t ask consistently. The crew forgets, the office is busy, and a generic review-request blast gets ignored.

A real conversation, not a review blast

  1. AI follows up after the job by phone call or text and asks how it went, like a good office manager would.
  2. It drafts the review with the customer. Based on what they said, the AI suggests a review they can post as-is or edit.
  3. It sends the link while they’re still engaged. The suggested review and your Google review link arrive by text during the conversation.
  4. Unhappy customers go to the owner. If someone had a bad experience, the conversation is routed to you for a personal fix before it turns into a public review.

Every review answered for you

When reviews come in, LSA Command replies to each one under your business name with a quick, professional response. That shows future customers you’re paying attention and keeps your profile active.

Frequently asked questions

Do reviews affect Google LSA ranking?

Yes. Google lists review score and the number of reviews among the factors that affect Local Services Ads ranking.

Is it OK for the AI to suggest review text?

The AI drafts a review based on what the customer actually said, and the customer decides whether to post it, edit it, or write their own.

What happens if a customer is unhappy?

The conversation is routed to the owner so the problem can be fixed personally.
