A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you jump in.

Is this for agencies or local businesses?

This signup is for agency owners. You’ll use LSA Command to support your local business clients. If you’re a business owner, talk with the agency that referred you.

What can I access as soon as I sign up?

Your agency overview and introduction booking link. Client onboarding, phone numbers, integrations, and the full tools stay locked until our team approves your account after meeting with you.

When does billing start?

Creating an account doesn’t activate paid client services. Standard LSA Command pricing is $200 per month per client. We’ll confirm your setup and billing with you before activation.

Does the person who referred me get credit?

Yes. When you sign up through their referral link, we connect the referral to your account. They can earn 15% on qualifying payments for your clients while those payments continue. It doesn’t add a referral surcharge to your price.

Can I use my own agency branding?

LSA Command supports white-label options. We’ll walk through what your agency needs during your introduction and help you plan the setup.
