Blocked At The Carrier

The instant a call hits your line, it's screened against the Nomorobo database of known robocallers — built into our Twilio layer. 90%+ of spam is killed before your phone ever rings.

Anything that slips through gets answered by the AI. Pre-recorded messages, robotic voices, "press 9" prompts — flagged as spam on the spot. No notification fires. The call is logged in your dashboard tagged ROBOCALL BLOCKED — off your line and out of your lead count.

Every new spam pattern the AI hears becomes a new blocking rule — a trick works once, then it's blocked for every client, forever. The filter gets stronger with every call across the whole network.

Ultimate Response Defense

The LSA API Fallback Interceptor

The dirty secret of LSA message leads: replies travel back to Google over SMS. Your phone — or any generic AI agent — sees "delivered." But 10–15% of the time the reply never lands in the LSA thread and never reaches the customer. It happens to human replies and off-the-shelf AI bots alike, and the only way to even see it is to log into LSA and open the lead — so nobody ever knows. LSA Command is built on the LSA API: it watches the actual lead thread after every reply, and if your response hasn't landed within minutes, it force-pushes it through the API — a true 100% response rate.

Zero Dropped Leads

"Delivered" doesn't mean delivered. Google's SMS bridge silently drops replies before they reach the LSA thread. Our watchdog checks the actual thread after every response — if it isn't there within minutes, the API hard-injects it. Every recovered message shows in your dashboard as an exact count, not an estimate.

Preserve Your "Replies in Minutes" Badge

A 90% response rate versus a 100% rate can drop your ad rank overnight. We programmatically enforce a flawless score so you never lose the Google Local Services buy-box.

Why Every Other AI Agent Misses 10–15%

A generic AI agent bolted onto LSA replies over SMS and trusts the "delivered" receipt — it has no view inside the LSA thread, so it never knows the reply vanished. Catching these takes API-level delivery verification, which only exists in a system built for LSA from the ground up. Ours backs you up even if you answer messages yourself.
