Why electricians lose Google LSA leads

Electrical LSA leads range from “half my house has no power” to panel upgrades and EV charger installs. Many homeowners send a message instead of calling, especially for planned work. If those replies are slow, or silently fail to reach them, they move on to the next listing.

Electricians also work in attics, crawlspaces, and on commercial jobs where answering the phone isn’t realistic. That’s where responsiveness slips.

What LSA Command does for electricians

LSA Command handles the LSA leads for power outages and troubleshooting, panel upgrades, EV charger installation, lighting, and wiring repairs. You get the same system on every lead:

  • AI call answering: your team rings first, and AI picks up at 15 seconds so every call is answered inside Google’s 30-second window.
  • Message lead response: replies in under 30 seconds, verified through the LSA API so none silently drop.
  • Lead feedback and credits: every lead scored and rated back to Google; spam and invalid leads flagged for credit.
  • Service area targeting: the exact cities and zip codes you want, pushed to LSA in seconds, with your live rank on each one.
  • Review generation: AI follow-up after every job and a reply to every review.

What you get on every lead

Within seconds of every call or message, you get a text and an email with the customer’s number, the call recording, a short AI summary, and the full transcript. Google’s own alert for a phone lead is just an email. You call back already knowing what the job is.

Frequently asked questions

Are Google Local Services Ads worth it for electricians?

LSA puts electricians at the very top of Google with the Google Verified badge, and you pay per lead instead of per click. It pays off when you answer every lead fast, rate leads back to Google, and keep your service area tight. Those are the areas LSA Command handles.

Do message leads matter for electricians on LSA?

Yes, especially for planned projects like panel upgrades and EV chargers, where homeowners often message instead of calling. LSA Command replies in under 30 seconds and checks through the LSA API that each reply actually reached the customer.
