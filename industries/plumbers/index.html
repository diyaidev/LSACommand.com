Why plumbers lose Google LSA leads

Plumbing leads are urgent. A homeowner with water on the floor calls the first two or three listings and books whoever answers. If your crew is under a sink and the call goes to voicemail, that job is gone in minutes.

Plumbers also see plenty of low-value LSA traffic: wrong numbers, sales calls, and spam. Paying for those leads and never rating them back to Google teaches the system nothing.

What LSA Command does for plumbers

LSA Command handles the LSA leads for emergency leak repair, drain cleaning, water heater repair and replacement, sewer line work, and fixture installs. You get the same system on every lead:

  • AI call answering: your team rings first, and AI picks up at 15 seconds so every call is answered inside Google’s 30-second window.
  • Message lead response: replies in under 30 seconds, verified through the LSA API so none silently drop.
  • Lead feedback and credits: every lead scored and rated back to Google; spam and invalid leads flagged for credit.
  • Service area targeting: the exact cities and zip codes you want, pushed to LSA in seconds, with your live rank on each one.
  • Review generation: AI follow-up after every job and a reply to every review.

What you get on every lead

Within seconds of every call or message, you get a text and an email with the customer’s number, the call recording, a short AI summary, and the full transcript. Google’s own alert for a phone lead is just an email. You call back already knowing what the job is.

Frequently asked questions

Are Google Local Services Ads worth it for plumbers?

LSA puts plumbers at the very top of Google with the Google Verified badge, and you pay per lead instead of per click. It pays off when you answer every lead fast, rate leads back to Google, and keep your service area tight. Those are the areas LSA Command handles.

Can LSA Command tell an emergency plumbing call from a routine one?

The AI qualifies every caller and captures the problem, address, and urgency. Your alert includes a short summary, so you can see right away whether it’s a burst pipe or a quote request. Hot leads can be warm-transferred to your phone while the caller is still on the line.
