Why your LSA service area decides your lead quality

Google shows Local Services Ads to people searching inside the areas you choose, and closeness to the customer is one of the main ranking factors. If your area is too small, you miss profitable jobs. If it’s too loose, you pay for leads 50 minutes away.

Setting it up by hand in the LSA dashboard is slow, especially for contractors who serve dozens of towns. Many accounts end up with duplicate areas, missing suburbs, or old zones nobody remembers adding.

Build the exact footprint you want

  • Find every profitable area. The Radius tool lists every city, county, and zip code in your region so you can include the ones you want and exclude the ones you don’t.
  • Clean up duplicates. Overlapping and duplicate areas are found and removed in one click.
  • Push it to LSA instantly. No manual entry. Your finished service area uploads straight to your Google LSA account through the API.
  • See what each change produced. Every radius change is logged and compared against lead volume, so you know whether an expansion actually paid off.

Your real LSA rank on every city you serve

The LSA Command Rank Tracker drops a numbered pin on each city in your service area, showing your live Local Services position there for your main keyword. Your home base is tracked weekly and your whole area monthly. Rank-over-time charts show whether faster response and more reviews are moving you up.

Frequently asked questions

Can I target specific zip codes in Google Local Services Ads?

Yes. LSA lets you choose service areas by city, county, and zip code. LSA Command makes it fast to build that list and push it to your account.

Will a bigger service area get me more LSA leads?

It can, but proximity is a ranking factor, so far-away areas usually rank lower and may bring poorer-fit jobs. The goal is the most profitable footprint, not the biggest one.

How do I know where I rank in each city?

The LSA Command Rank Tracker shows your live LSA position on every city in your service area, tracked on your main keyword.
